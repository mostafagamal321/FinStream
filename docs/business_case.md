# Business Case

FinStream solves delayed financial decision-making by processing three types of financial data: transactions, market prices, and company news.

- Transaction data is used to identify suspicious payment behavior.
- Historical transaction patterns are used to build risk baselines and train fraud models.
- Stock data is used to detect abnormal price movement, volume spikes, and volatility.
- News data is used to extract company sentiment and explain market movement.

The datasets are not necessarily connected row by row. They are connected at the platform and business-domain level: FinStream is a shared real-time financial intelligence platform serving multiple financial use cases.
